About

Kimberly Krupa, Ph.D.

Director of Network Engagement and Communications at the Florida College Access Network. First-generation college graduate.

I build the communications infrastructure that turns data into public will and public will into children's funding. I've run ballot campaigns, founded collective impact networks, led newsrooms, and spent five years as a doctoral researcher studying why communities thrive or fail after disaster. That history shapes how I work: I start with the story in the data, and I don't stop until it reaches the people who can act on it.

Formerly Executive Director of Achieve Escambia (StriveTogether network). Founded the Earn and Learn Career Pathways Program (now NOLA CCAN, the New Orleans College and Career Attainment Network) at Tulane University in 2013. University of New Orleans Ph.D. and M.S. in Urban Studies. B.A. in Journalism, The College of New Jersey.
