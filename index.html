About

Kimberly Krupa, Ph.D.

I started as an education reporter. I stayed because the story kept getting bigger.

Twenty-five years later, I’ve led newsrooms, run ballot campaigns, built collective impact networks from scratch, and spent five years as a doctoral researcher studying why communities survive disaster or don’t. Each chapter taught me the same thing: the data already contains the argument. My job is finding it and making sure the right people hear it.

Founder and Executive Director of Achieve Escambia (StriveTogether Cradle to Career Network member). Founder of Louisiana’s first place-based college access initiative, now the New Orleans College and Career Attainment Network. Ph.D. and M.S. in Urban Studies, University of New Orleans. B.A. in Journalism, The College of New Jersey. Member, Education Writers Association. First-generation college graduate.
