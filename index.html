About

Kimberly Krupa, Ph.D.

Director of Network Engagement and Communications at the Florida College Access Network, hosted by the University of South Florida System. First-generation college graduate.

I build the communications infrastructure that turns data into public will and public will into children's funding. I've run ballot campaigns, founded collective impact networks, led newsrooms, and spent five years as a doctoral researcher studying why communities thrive or fail after disaster. That history shapes how I work: I start with the story in the data, and I don't stop until it reaches the people who can act on it.

Formerly Executive Director of Achieve Escambia (StriveTogether network). Founded the New Orleans College and Career Attainment Network at Tulane. University of New Orleans Ph.D. and M.d. in Urban Studies. B.A. in Journalism.
